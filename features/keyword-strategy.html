Keyword Strategy ​

Choose the search terms you want to track and decide what each keyword is for. Show targets guide your podcast's metadata; episode targets guide individual episodes. Tracking and assigning are separate: a keyword can be measured before you choose where to use it.

To access: Search Visibility → Strategy. The other tabs are Organic Visibility · Rankings · Tracker.

Add a keyword ​

  1. Type a listener's search phrase into Add a keyword.
  2. Review related ideas when available, or track the exact phrase you entered.
  3. If Word variations appears, review the selected variations before using Track group. Each newly tracked variation uses a keyword slot; already-tracked terms aren't added twice.
  4. Check Used for. Adding while Show is selected assigns the term to your show; otherwise use Assign to choose its purpose.

Try a suggestion offers a starting point if you don't know what to search. If related search ideas are temporarily unavailable, you can still track the phrase you typed.

Decide what each keyword is for ​

TargetWhen to use itWhat PodSEO checks
ShowA topic your whole podcast should be found forShow title, author, and description
EpisodeA topic of a planned or published episodeThat episode's title and description
GuestThe name of a guest on an episodeTracked, without scoring the name against your metadata
BrandYour show or host nameBranded rankings, without scoring
CompetitorA phrase you want to benchmark against another showTracked, without scoring
ThemeA set of terms you want to organize togetherGrouping, without a separate metadata score
UnassignedA term you're still exploringTracked; can also contribute to show-description alignment and episode checks as described below

A keyword can serve your show and several episodes at the same time. Adding a Brand, Guest, Competitor or Theme target does not cancel an existing Show or Episode target. Deleting the keyword removes it from tracking; removing an assignment leaves it tracked.

Assign a keyword without losing its tracking ​

  1. Open Assign on a keyword row and choose The show.
  2. Find the episode the term also describes and select it. Choices save immediately.
  3. Close the chooser to inspect both chips.
  4. Remove one chip with ×. The other assignment and the tracked keyword stay in place.

For guest names, choose the episode and mark the keyword as a guest. In Planner's episode panel the option is It's a guest's name.

Show keywords ​

Start with two or three terms that honestly describe the show. The guidance is to aim for about five, with two in the show title; this isn't a separate cap. Review the result in Command Center → Show optimization, where you work on show title, author, description, and cover.

Episode keywords ​

Choose specific terms the episode actually covers, usually two or three. Add them here or open the episode's Keywords for this episode panel in Planner. Episode keywords are available on every plan, including Free. A guest name is tracked separately from the terms used for the episode's score.

If an episode has no targets of its own, its checks use your show keywords. If no show keywords are set either, they use your Unassigned keywords. Assign episode targets when a particular episode has a more specific focus.

Unassigned keywords ​

Use this as an inbox for future topics or experiments. Unassigned means no specific purpose has been chosen; it does not exclude the term from all scoring. These terms can contribute to show-description alignment and suggestions, and to episode checks when neither episode nor show keywords are set. When a term already appears in an episode, PodSEO can suggest Use for this episode. Review the proposed episode before accepting it.

Find keywords from your content ​

Choose AI suggestions to review recommendations based on your show's content and positioning. Suggestions are grouped by purpose. Review each phrase before using Add or Add all. An episode suggestion can remain Unassigned until you choose the episode it belongs to.

The panel offers Generate new suggestions when your plan and current allowance permit it. Existing suggestions stay available if another generation isn't available yet.

Find keywords from actual listening and viewing ​

Choose From your analytics. This view brings together real searches that produced YouTube views and the topics of episodes receiving Spotify search impressions. Read the Evidence for each phrase: a Spotify episode topic isn't an exact search query reported by Spotify.

The view compares the available audio search positions and groups terms into Open ground on audio, Checking positions, and Already found on audio where supported. Use Track to monitor a useful term, then assign it to your show or an episode.

If a source is missing, the panel offers a connection link. See Analytics Connections. Available detail follows your source data and plan, including YouTube's Free restrictions.

Check what's missing from your metadata ​

Open Strategy checks for your show-keyword coverage and episodes without their own targets. Each row also has a coverage verdict:

  • Show keywords indicate whether the phrase appears in the title or author, description only, or is missing.
  • Episode keywords show how many assigned episode titles contain the term.
  • Brand, guest, and competitor terms are measured without being scored.

Click a verdict for its breakdown. Needs attention filters show and episode keywords missing from the titles they serve. This is where the former Keyword Coverage work now lives.

Filter and organize ​

Use All keywords, Show, Episodes, Brand & guests, Competitors, Themes, Unassigned, Needs attention, AI suggestions, or From your analytics, as available. Episode, competitor, and theme filters can narrow further to a specific item. The Themes column and All themes filter help organize related terms.

On larger screens, select multiple rows to use the bulk assignment or removal actions. Use Help near the title for the in-app explanation of targets.

Read keyword measurements ​

Search volume is roughly how many monthly searches a term gets. The six-step meter uses these ranges:

LevelSearches per month
Very nicheUnder 100
Niche100–250
Small250–1K
Medium1K–2.5K
Popular2.5K–10K
Very popular10K+

Hover or focus the meter for its range. A clock means the measurement isn't available yet. A niche phrase may be a better fit for your show than a crowded popular one.

Difficulty describes how hard it is to compete, by platform. Popularity describes how crowded the term is; it isn't search volume. Read them together with relevance instead of chasing the largest number.

Keyword allowance ​

Your account has one tracked-keyword pool shared across all podcasts: Free 5 · Podcaster 100 · Producer 500 · Agency 50,000. Assigning a tracked keyword to several targets doesn't use another keyword slot. Adding new terms or word variations does.

Review results ​

Use Organic Visibility → Show / Episodes to discover terms already ranking, Rankings → Show / Episodes to compare tracked terms across platforms, and Tracker for their history. Keep an experiment long enough to compare its results, and note the dates of live metadata changes. PodSEO doesn't change your host's metadata when you assign a keyword.

Draft podcasts Ready soon ​

For a draft, Strategy is the available Search Visibility tab. Set show and episode targets before launch; live ranking results follow the published show. See Pre-launch Planning.

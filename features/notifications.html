Notifications ​

Stay Informed About Important Changes ​

Notifications keep you up to date on significant events related to your podcast — chart appearances, editorial features, PR mentions, competitor activity, trend alerts, and more. Instead of checking every section manually, notifications bring the most important updates to you.

To access: Click Notifications in the sidebar.

What Gets Notified ​

PodSEO sends notifications for events that are likely to require your attention:

  • Chart appearances — When your podcast enters a platform chart
  • Editorial features — When your podcast is added to a curated or editorial list
  • PR mentions — When your podcast is picked up by a new PR or media source
  • Pulse alerts — Trending news and topics relevant to your podcast's niche
  • Competitor activity — Notable chart, editorial, and PR changes from podcasts you're tracking
  • System updates — Important changes to your account or PodSEO features

Managing Notifications ​

You can configure which notifications you receive and how you receive them from Settings → Notifications. This lets you focus on the signals that matter most to your workflow without being overwhelmed by updates.

Integration with Other Features ​

Notifications surface key moments from across PodSEO, including insights from Search Visibility, Competition, and Pulse. For a more comprehensive view, visit the Command Center which aggregates signals, tasks, and insights in one dashboard.

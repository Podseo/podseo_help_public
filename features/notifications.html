Notifications ​

Stay Informed About Important Changes ​

Notifications keep you up to date on significant events related to your podcast — chart appearances, editorial features, PR mentions, competitor activity, trend alerts, podcast health, social clips and posts, and more. Instead of checking every section manually, notifications bring the most important updates to you.

To access: Click Notifications in the sidebar.

Choose how alerts reach you ​

Open Settings → Notifications. Each notification type has separate controls for In-app, Email, and Browser where that delivery channel is supported. A dash means that channel is not available for that type.

  1. Find the type of event you want to adjust.
  2. Toggle in-app or browser delivery independently.
  3. For emails that support digests, choose As it happens, Daily digest, Weekly digest, or Never. Other email types have an on/off switch.
  4. Look for Notification settings saved. Reload to check that your choices remain selected.

If you haven't changed a setting, most notification types are emailed in your Weekly digest. A few arrive right away by default because they're time-sensitive: announcements, social post results, social account reconnect alerts, and podcast health alerts. New-episode insights default to the Daily digest so you get them while the episode is fresh.

Turning off email for an event does not turn off its in-app alerts. Digests combine updates on the selected cadence; use immediate delivery for events you want to act on quickly. Browser delivery also depends on your browser allowing PodSEO notifications.

The Emails from PodSEO → Tips & offers switch controls setup tips, upgrade offers, and LUCIA notes sent by email. It is separate from the per-event controls. Account, billing, and security messages still reach you.

What Gets Notified ​

PodSEO sends notifications for events that are likely to require your attention.

Your podcast's wins and health

  • Chart appearances — When your podcast enters a platform chart
  • Editorial features — When your podcast is added to a curated or editorial list
  • PR mentions — When your podcast is picked up by a new PR or media source
  • New-episode insight — A short read on how your newest episode is set up for discovery (paid plans)
  • Podcast health check — A periodic summary of your show's distribution and feed health
  • Podcast health alert — An urgent alert when your feed stops working or your show is removed from a listening platform

Competitors

  • Competitor activity — New chart appearances, editorial lists, and PR mentions for podcasts you're tracking

Trends

  • Pulse news — News stories relevant to your podcast's niche
  • Pulse Google Trends ideas — Trending Google searches that match your keywords, as timely episode ideas

Social Publishing

  • Clip highlights ready — When PodSEO has found the best moments in an episode for social clips
  • Clip video ready — When a new social clip has finished generating
  • Social post results — Whether your scheduled or published posts went out successfully
  • Social account needs reconnecting — When a connected social account needs to be reconnected before PodSEO can post to it

Your account

  • Monthly credits refreshed — When your monthly credit allowance resets
  • Plan changes — When your subscription changes
  • Announcements — Important updates about PodSEO features

The Notifications Page ​

The Notifications page lists your full notification history across all your podcasts. Switch between Unread and All, and use the podcast filter to focus on a single show. Click Mark all read to clear your unread notifications (when a podcast filter is applied, the button marks just that podcast's notifications as read).

Your Weekly Email ​

Eligible accounts can receive a Monday ranking recap, with improvements, declines, new rankings and keywords close to the top five. Accounts receiving LUCIA's weekly coaching note receive that note instead of the ranking recap. Delivery depends on your email preferences, a verified email address and recent activity; tracking keywords is needed for the ranking section. Notifications you set to Weekly digest still follow their own delivery preferences.

Integration with Other Features ​

Notifications surface key moments from across PodSEO, including insights from Search Visibility, Competition, and Pulse. For a more comprehensive view, visit the Home which aggregates signals, tasks, and insights in one dashboard.

Troubleshoot a missing notification ​

Check the event's delivery settings, your browser permission, and your email spam folder. A daily or weekly digest will not arrive immediately. Features still follow your plan's access rules, and the absence of an alert does not prove that a ranking or placement has not changed—open its feature page to inspect the available data.

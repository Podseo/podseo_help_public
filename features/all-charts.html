All Charts ​

Browse the Charts Your Show Could Climb ​

All Charts lets you explore podcast charts across Apple Podcasts, Spotify, YouTube, and Amazon Music. Pick a platform, a country, and a chart to see its full ranking. Use this intelligence to understand your competitive landscape, learn from chart-performing podcasts, and spot the charts where your podcast could break through.

To access: Measure → Charts → All Charts.

What You Can Discover ​

Cross-Platform Intelligence ​

All Charts gives you insight into what's charting across all major platforms:

  • Apple Podcasts: The largest podcast platform's charts by category and country
    • Top Subscriber Shows ranks shows by their paid subscriptions through Apple Podcasts Subscriptions, and Top Series ranks limited series (shows with a set run of episodes). Apple publishes them in some countries only, so they appear in the chart list only where they exist.
  • Spotify: Spotify's podcast charts
  • YouTube: YouTube's podcast charts
  • Amazon Music: Amazon's podcast chart rankings

Full Ranking Tables ​

For any chart you choose, you can see the complete ranking — the podcasts featured and the order they appear in. The header shows how many podcasts are in the chart and an Updated date so you know how current the ranking is. If your own show is on the chart, its row is highlighted with a Your podcast badge so you can spot it instantly. This lets you study who's charting in a given category and country and where your podcast would fit.

How to Use All Charts ​

All Charts is built around a simple three-step selection:

  1. Pick a platform - Choose Apple Podcasts, Spotify, YouTube, or Amazon Music. Only countries and charts available for that platform stay selectable.
  2. Pick a country - Select the country whose charts you want to explore.
  3. Pick a chart - Choose a specific chart, and its full ranking table loads below.

From there you can read the ranking, see which podcasts are featured, and compare across platforms and countries by changing your selections.

How to Use Chart Intelligence for Growth ​

Identifying Your Opportunities ​

All Charts helps you find realistic opportunities for your podcast to break through:

  • Category analysis: Explore different charts to find where your content could compete effectively
  • Regional markets: Discover countries where your type of content performs well but might have less competition
  • Gap analysis: Spot charts or categories where there's audience demand but limited quality content

Learning from Success ​

Use chart data to understand what's actually working:

  • Content patterns: Analyze what types of episodes and topics chart-performing podcasts are creating
  • Format insights: Understand which podcast formats and lengths perform best in a given chart
  • Consistency patterns: See how often successful podcasts release content
  • Positioning strategies: Learn how top podcasts position themselves within their categories

Strategic Applications for Growth ​

Content Strategy Development ​

Use chart intelligence to guide your content decisions:

  • Topic validation: See which topics are resonating with audiences in your category
  • Format optimization: Understand what episode lengths and formats perform best
  • Release strategy: Identify timing patterns among the podcasts that are charting

Competitive Positioning ​

Charts reveal strategic opportunities in your market:

  • Differentiation strategy: Find ways to stand out from what's already successful
  • Partnership opportunities: Identify potential collaborators who are achieving chart success
  • Market gaps: Discover underserved niches where you could establish leadership
  • Benchmark setting: Set realistic but ambitious goals based on comparable podcast performance

Best Practices for Chart Research ​

Regular Market Intelligence ​

  • Weekly reviews: Check charts regularly to stay current with trends and opportunities
  • Category focus: Pay special attention to charts in your primary category
  • Cross-platform monitoring: Understand how charts differ across Apple Podcasts, Spotify, YouTube, and Amazon Music
  • Competitor tracking: Monitor how your direct competitors are performing in the rankings

Strategic Implementation ​

  • Content inspiration: Use chart-performing content as inspiration for your own episodes, not for copying
  • Timing optimization: Plan your content releases around patterns you observe in chart performance
  • Performance goals: Set chart-based targets that are ambitious but achievable for your category
  • Platform strategy: Tailor your approach to each platform based on what charts well there

Integration with Your Growth Strategy ​

All Charts works best when combined with your other optimization efforts. Use chart intelligence to inform your keyword strategy, validate your content direction against what's actually succeeding, and set competitive benchmarks that guide your SEO and content optimization priorities.

To see which charts your own podcast already appears on — and how your rank on each has moved over time — use My Charts. All Charts is for exploring the wider landscape; My Charts is for tracking your own placements.

Getting Started: Begin by selecting your podcast's platform and country, then open a chart in your category to understand the competitive landscape. Identify 2-3 successful podcasts you can learn from, and use those insights to guide your content strategy and set realistic but ambitious performance goals.

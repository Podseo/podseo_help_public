Curated Lists ​

Browse Platform-Curated Podcast Collections ​

Curated Lists lets you explore curated podcast collections from major platforms. Browse editorial features from Apple Podcasts, Spotify, Amazon Music, and YouTube Music to understand what platforms are promoting and discover strategic opportunities.

To access: Promote → PR Outreach, then the Curated lists tab.

What Are Editorial Lists? ​

Podcast platforms maintain editorial teams that curate collections of recommended podcasts. These collections appear in prominent positions within each platform's app, driving significant discovery for featured podcasts. Examples include:

  • Apple Podcasts' "New & Noteworthy" and themed collections
  • Spotify's editorial playlists and featured podcast sections
  • Amazon Music's curated podcast recommendations
  • YouTube Music's podcast highlights

PodSEO tracks these editorial features across all major platforms, giving you visibility into what's being promoted.

How to Use Curated Lists ​

Browse by Platform ​

Use the platform chips at the top to choose a platform, and the editorial lists for that platform load below:

  • Apple Podcasts - Editorial collections, New & Noteworthy, category features
  • Spotify - Editorial playlists, genre features, trending selections
  • Amazon Music - Curated collections and platform recommendations
  • YouTube Music - Featured podcast content

Switch between platforms to see what each one is currently promoting.

Country: use the Country selector to choose from the countries available for that platform. The initial view is the United States. Editorial lists can differ by market, so compare the same country as your listening app. This selection is separate from keyword search countries in Settings → Shows.

Explore Individual Lists ​

Each editorial list shows:

  • The list name
  • The podcasts currently featured on it
  • The platform it belongs to

Strategic Applications ​

Competitive Intelligence ​

Understanding editorial placement helps you:

  • See which competitors receive editorial features
  • Identify patterns in what platforms choose to promote
  • Spot gaps where your podcast could fit

Content Planning ​

Editorial lists reveal platform priorities:

  • Seasonal themes and trending topics
  • Content types that receive promotion
  • Categories with active editorial support

Opportunity Identification ​

Use editorial research to guide your strategy:

  • Find editorial lists relevant to your podcast's topic
  • Understand the quality and style of featured podcasts
  • Identify lists where your podcast could potentially appear

Connection to My Placements ​

Curated Lists shows the broader landscape of platform editorial content. My Placements shows where your specific podcast has been featured.

Use both together:

  1. Curated Lists - Research what's being promoted across platforms
  2. My Placements - Track your own editorial appearances

Requesting Editorial Features ​

While editorial placement isn't guaranteed, platforms do accept feature requests. For Apple Podcasts, use their official feature request form and follow their optimization guide.

Key tips for editorial consideration:

  • Submit requests at least 2 weeks before your desired feature date
  • Prepare special artwork following platform templates
  • Have a compelling story about why your content deserves promotion
  • Maintain consistently high content quality

Availability ​

Curated Lists is available to all users. The data updates regularly as PodSEO monitors editorial content across platforms.

Related Features: Check My Placements to see your own podcast's editorial appearances, and use All Charts to track chart rankings alongside editorial visibility.

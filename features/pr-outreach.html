PR Outreach ​

Manage Your Podcast Pitch Pipeline ​

PR Outreach provides a Kanban-style CRM for tracking your podcast promotion efforts. Manage pitch targets, track follow-ups, and move opportunities through your pipeline from initial contact to successful feature.

To access: Promote → PR Outreach in the sidebar. The page opens on the Pipeline tab; Curated lists and Placements are the other two tabs.

Your Outreach Pipeline ​

The pipeline organizes your outreach efforts into five stages:

Identified ​

Targets you've discovered but haven't contacted yet. These are opportunities waiting for your first pitch — editorial contacts, newsletter curators, podcast directories, or media outlets.

Contacted ​

Pitches you've sent that are awaiting response. When you move a card here, PodSEO records the pitch date automatically.

Replied ​

Active conversations where you've received a response. These are warm leads that need nurturing toward a feature.

Featured ​

Success! Targets that have featured your podcast. The feature date is recorded automatically when you move a card here.

Declined ​

Opportunities that didn't work out. Keeping these helps you track conversion rates and learn from what didn't resonate.

Pipeline Features ​

Summary Dashboard ​

At the top, a funnel summarizes your progress across four key stages at a glance:

  • Identified - How many targets are ready to pitch
  • Contacted - Active outreach awaiting responses
  • Replied - Conversations in progress
  • Featured - Successful features achieved

The funnel focuses on these four stages. Declined still appears as its own column on the board below, so you can keep a record of opportunities that didn't work out, but it isn't part of the funnel summary.

Overdue Follow-ups ​

An alert appears when you have follow-ups past their scheduled date. Timely follow-ups are crucial for successful outreach - don't let opportunities go cold.

Target Cards ​

Each pipeline card shows:

  • Target name and domain
  • Current status
  • Any notes you've added
  • Scheduled follow-up date

Drag-and-Drop Management ​

Move targets between columns as your relationship progresses. Drag a card from "Contacted" to "Replied" when you get a response, or to "Featured" when you achieve success.

Managing Your Targets ​

Adding Targets ​

Two ways to add pitch targets:

Add Custom Target - Create your own entry for any newsletter, blog, editor, or award submission you want to pitch. The form asks for:

  • Target name (required) - The person or publication
  • Website URL
  • Description - A brief note on what this target is
  • Contact name, Email, and Twitter handle
  • Submission page - The URL where you submit your pitch

Once the card is created, open it to add notes about the opportunity.

Find Opportunities - Opens a panel with PodSEO's curated list of pitch targets for editorial, award, and featured placement consideration. This includes platform editorial submission pages (such as Apple Podcasts Editorial and Spotify Editorial) alongside podcast newsletters, directories, blogs, and curated recommendation sites worth pitching — for example, Goodpods, EarBuds Podcast Collective, and All Hear. Click Add on any opportunity to create a card in your Identified column. Each opportunity includes a description and, where available, the submission or contact link you'll need to pitch your podcast.

Target Details ​

Open a target card to inspect its available contact and submission links. Add Notes about the pitch, set a Follow-up reminder, and choose the Current status that matches what actually happened. Notes save when you leave the field. The details also show when the target was added, pitched and featured.

Move a card to Contacted after sending your pitch yourself. Changing its status records progress; it does not send an email. The follow-up date helps you keep track of the next action.

Daily Follow-up Email ​

On days when you have follow-ups that are overdue or due that day, PodSEO sends you a single daily digest email listing them, split into overdue and due-today items, with a link back to your pipeline. You won't get the email on days with nothing due. The digest respects your email preferences, so if you've opted out of PodSEO emails you won't receive it.

Outreach Best Practices ​

Building Your Pipeline ​

  • Start with research - Identify publications, newsletters, and platforms that feature podcasts like yours
  • Quality over quantity - Focus on relevant targets rather than mass outreach
  • Mix target types - Include editorial contacts, newsletter curators, and industry publications

Effective Pitching ​

  • Personalize each pitch - Reference specific content or explain why you're a good fit
  • Be concise - Busy editors appreciate brief, clear pitches
  • Include value - Explain what's in it for their audience
  • Make it easy - Provide assets, links, and everything they need

Following Up ​

  • Schedule systematically - Set follow-up dates for every contact
  • Be persistent but respectful - 2-3 follow-ups is appropriate
  • Add value each time - Share news, new episodes, or relevant angles
  • Know when to move on - Not every pitch will convert

Learning from Success ​

Track what works in your Featured column:

  • Which pitch angles resonated
  • What types of targets converted
  • Timing patterns that worked
  • Content that attracted features

Integration with My Placements ​

PR Outreach helps you actively pursue features, while My Placements tracks where features appear. Together they give you a complete picture of your editorial visibility efforts and results.

PR Outreach and AI Visibility ​

A relevant newsletter, review site, or podcast roundup can introduce your show to new readers. Some web pages are also cited by AI assistants. In AI Mentions, inspect cited sources and save a relevant page to your outreach pipeline. A placement does not guarantee that an assistant will recommend your podcast.

Availability ​

PR Outreach is available on paid plans (Podcaster, Producer, and Agency). The pipeline helps you systematically grow your podcast's media presence.

Related Features: Check My Placements to see where your podcast has been featured, use Curated Lists to research editorial opportunities, and track the payoff in AI Mentions as web recommendations feed AI discovery.

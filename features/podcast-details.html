Show details ​

Your Show at a Glance ​

Show details (formerly the Overview tab) is your starting point for understanding how your podcast is performing. It pulls your most important signals — visibility, AI recommendations, search rankings, distribution, listener sentiment, and chart presence — into a single grid of summary widgets. Each widget shows the headline number and gives you a clear path into the detailed page behind it.

To access: Click Home in the sidebar and scroll to Show details at the bottom of the page, or click the Show details link under the status line at the top of Home. The section loads when you reach it. Old links to the Overview page open this section.

Visibility Score ​

The most prominent widget is your Visibility Score — a number on a 0-10 scale that measures how discoverable your podcast is across Apple Podcasts, Spotify, Amazon Music, and YouTube Music. Higher scores mean your show is easier to find.

The score combines your search rankings, chart positions, and editorial and qualifying web placements, with adjustments for the show's recorded activity and Apple listing status. Metadata alignment scores are separate checks; improving them does not automatically add visibility points.

Alongside the score you'll see:

  • Score change — how much your score moved over the last 10 days (for example, +0.12 or -0.05)
  • Peer benchmark — the average visibility of comparable podcasts, plus how far ahead of your peers you are. If there isn't enough positioning data to build a reliable peer group, you'll see Needs more data instead; adding clearer Subject or Audience topics in Positioning helps. While the comparison is being refreshed you'll see Updating — your score itself is still current.
  • Rank — where you stand among all indexed podcasts (a lower rank number means better discoverability). An arrow next to your rank shows how many positions you've climbed (up) or dropped (down). New shows see Pending until a rank is available.

A score trend chart tracks your measured visibility over time. When a notable change occurs, a short explanation helps you identify signals worth checking; it does not prove which action caused the change.

Score and rank can move differently. Your global rank compares an average of recent scores, over roughly the past week, with other indexed shows. It can change even when your latest score is steady. Search can also change when the chart visibility of shows appearing alongside yours changes the weight of a keyword.

Click View analysis to open Visibility Progress and inspect each component over time. The component cards use separate display scales; they are not shares that add up to the total. See Visibility Score Explained for the distinction.

AI Visibility ​

When AI recommendation data is available, the AI Visibility widget shows how often ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini recommend your podcast when listeners ask about your topics. Each assistant has its own row showing whether it currently mentions your show and for how many topics.

Click View analysis to open the full breakdown in AI Mentions.

Keyword Positions ​

The Keyword Positions widget tracks how many of your keywords sit in each ranking bucket over time, so you can watch your search footprint grow. Your tracked keywords are grouped into four position bands:

  • Top 3 — your strongest, most visible rankings
  • 4-10 — keywords ranking on the first page of results
  • 11-50 — keywords with room to climb
  • 50-100 — keywords on the edge of visibility

Seeing more keywords move into the Top 3 and 4-10 bands over time is a strong sign your optimization efforts are working. Click View analysis to explore your full keyword rankings in Show Visibility.

Distribution ​

The Distribution widget shows where your show is listed and whether each listing is healthy. It covers your RSS feed plus your presence on Apple Podcasts, Spotify, Amazon Music, and YouTube Music, with a status indicator for each so you can spot problems at a glance.

If PodSEO doesn't detect a dedicated website for your show, this widget also flags it, since a custom website gives your podcast its own Google-indexed pages that platforms alone can't provide.

For a full diagnostic — feed analysis, platform listings, website setup, and feed performance — open the Distribution tab.

Reviews, Featured Placements, Top Charts, and Ratings ​

The lower section of the grid loads a set of summary panels that give you a quick read on listener sentiment and external visibility:

  • Reviews — your most recent listener reviews
  • Featured placements — a summary of your editorial visibility across platforms
  • Top charts — your current chart presence
  • Ratings — your star ratings on Apple Podcasts and Spotify

Each panel links through to its detailed page so you can dig deeper — your reviews and ratings in Ratings & Reviews, your editorial placements in My Placements, and your chart standings in My Charts.

Show details is designed to be the first place you check each day. Scan the headline numbers to see what's changed, then use the View analysis links to jump straight into whichever area needs your attention.

When your show qualifies, use Share Cards to share its PodSEO visibility achievement with a public profile link or downloadable image.

Show Rankings ​

Track Your Keyword Rankings Across All Platforms ​

Show Rankings lets you monitor how your podcast ranks for specific keywords across Apple Podcasts, Spotify, Amazon Music, and YouTube Music. Review tracked keywords across platforms, organize them into groups, and inspect their history. Use Strategy to add or assign terms.

To access: Search Visibility → Rankings → Show. Show and Episodes are modes of the single Rankings tab. Older links called this Show Rankings or Platform Rankings.

What You'll See ​

Multi-Platform Ranking Table ​

The main view shows your tracked keywords with rankings for each platform:

  • Keyword - The search term you're tracking
  • Groups - The themes the keyword belongs to, shown read-only; manage memberships in Strategy
  • Apple Podcasts (AP) - Current rank and change indicator
  • Spotify (SP) - Current rank and change indicator
  • Amazon Music (AM) - Current rank and change indicator
  • YouTube Music (YM) - Current rank and change indicator

Each platform column shows:

  • Your current position (e.g., #8)
  • An arrow showing whether the position improved or fell over the selected comparison period, when an earlier position is available
  • Blank if not ranking for that keyword

Ranking Chart ​

Above the table, a line chart shows the ranking history of one keyword at a time. Select a keyword in the table and the chart plots its position on all four platforms—Apple Podcasts, Spotify, Amazon Music, and YouTube Music—as separate lines over your chosen timeframe, so you can compare platforms side by side.

Smart Groups ​

Quick filters to find keywords by performance:

  • All Keywords - Your complete tracking list
  • Top 10 - Keywords where you rank in the top 10
  • Rising - Keywords with improving rankings
  • Dropping - Keywords losing positions
  • New (7 days) - Recently added keywords
  • Not Ranking - Keywords where you don't appear in results

Custom Groups ​

The Groups column displays your existing themes with their colors. Group membership is read-only in Rankings; use Manage keywords in Strategy to create themes or change assignments.

Managing Your Keywords ​

Adding Keywords ​

Click Manage keywords in Strategy to add or assign keywords. Consider tracking:

  • Your show keywords (the core topics of your positioning)
  • Competitor keywords you want to rank for
  • Topic keywords in your niche
  • Branded terms (your podcast name, host names)

Manage Groups in Strategy ​

  1. Click Manage keywords in Strategy.
  2. Use the keyword's Themes control or Assign → Theme to organize it.
  3. Return to Rankings → Show to review the updated Groups column.

A keyword can belong to more than one theme. Changing a theme does not create another tracked keyword.

Compare the same keyword over time ​

  1. Select a keyword to draw its history across the four platforms.
  2. Start with 15 days, then choose 90 days for a longer view.
  3. Read the same row's change indicators alongside the chart. The comparison period changes; the keyword stays the same.

An earlier position must be available to calculate a change. A dash or a missing comparison is not a download count.

Exporting Data ​

Click Download CSV to export your keyword data for external analysis or reporting.

Over Your Keyword Limit ​

If you're tracking more keywords than your plan allows (for example, after changing plans), a warning appears above the table: "You're tracking more keywords than your plan allows." Upgrade to raise your keyword limit and get complete data, or remove keywords you no longer need.

Understanding Your Rankings ​

Reading the change indicators ​

The change indicator compares the latest position with an earlier position for the selected comparison period:

  • Up arrow - Ranking improved (moved up)
  • Down arrow - Ranking dropped (moved down)
  • No arrow - Position unchanged, or no comparable earlier position is available

Platform Differences ​

Rankings often vary by platform:

  • A keyword ranking #5 on Apple might be #15 on Spotify
  • Some platforms may not rank your podcast for certain keywords
  • Focus optimization where you have the most potential

Country Differences ​

The Show Rankings table shows positions as a listener in the United States sees them. A keyword can rank very differently in other markets; to compare countries, add search countries for the podcast in Settings → Shows (paid plans, with a per-podcast allowance of additional countries) and use the country selector in Keyword Tracker — that's where per-country rankings are shown. Adding countries doesn't change this table.

Choose your next action ​

Use Dropping to find terms worth investigating and Top 10 to review established positions. Compare related metadata edits with ranking history, while remembering that timing alone does not prove what caused a change. Manage targets in Strategy, and use Tracker for country comparisons and competitor history.

Integration with Other Features ​

  • Keyword Strategy - Keywords from your strategy appear here
  • Show Visibility - Discover keywords you rank for organically
  • Keyword Tracker - Alternative view focused on individual keyword history

Availability ​

Show Rankings is available on paid plans. Free users see a limited preview with sample keywords.

Related Features: Use Keyword Strategy to build your keyword plan, Show Visibility to discover organic rankings, and Episode Rankings for episode-level tracking.

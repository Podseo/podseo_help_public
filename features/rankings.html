Rankings ​

Compare search positions for the keywords you already track. Rankings brings the show and episode views together, so you can check whether a phrase finds your whole podcast, particular episodes, or both.

To access: Get found → Search Visibility → Rankings, then select Show or Episodes. These are modes of one tab, rather than separate top-level tabs.

Choose the right view ​

ModeWhat it answersFull guide
ShowWhere does my podcast rank for each tracked keyword across the listening platforms?Show rankings
EpisodesWhich episodes rank for each tracked keyword on Apple Podcasts and Spotify?Episode rankings

Start with Show to compare platforms. Switch to Episodes to see which content is carrying a term. A dash or an empty result means there is no observed rank for that item in the view; it isn't a traffic count.

Rankings, Search Appearances, or Tracker? ​

  • Strategy is where you choose and assign your keywords.
  • Search Appearances discovers terms your show or episodes already rank for, including terms you haven't chosen to track.
  • Rankings compares positions for your tracked terms in Show or Episodes mode.
  • Tracker follows the history of a tracked keyword, with platform, country, and timeframe filters and competitor comparisons.

Use Strategy to add a missing term. Tracking a keyword doesn't assign it to the show or an episode; choose its target there when you want it to guide optimization.

Availability ​

The full Show and Episodes ranking views require a paid plan. Free users can use Tracker for their tracked terms and see the limited preview offered in Rankings.

Share Your Podcast's Achievement ​

When your show qualifies, PodSEO offers a share card highlighting its visibility rank or recent score growth. Use the card to celebrate progress with listeners or include the achievement in your promotional material.

Open and share the card ​

Go to Traction → Overview and click Share next to your Rank. A window titled Take a victory lap opens with a preview of your card. From there you can:

  • Choose Copy link to share your public profile.
  • Share to X / Twitter or LinkedIn, or open More options for WhatsApp, Threads, Bluesky, Telegram, Reddit, Facebook, or Email. Each option opens that service's own sharing flow with a post ready for you to edit.
  • Click Download PNG to save the card image, or Download for Instagram for a 1080×1080 square version that also works well in X and LinkedIn posts.

Review your caption before posting. A sharing shortcut opens the destination's sharing flow; it is separate from Social Publishing, which creates episode clips and schedules posts to connected accounts.

What the achievement means ​

Cards can highlight a qualifying position in PodSEO's overall visibility ranking or positive score growth over the preceding 90 days. This is a PodSEO visibility achievement, not an Apple or Spotify chart rank and not a download or audience-size claim. See Visibility Score Explained.

Why isn't a card available? ​

Eligibility depends on the show's rank or growth, its current activity status, and having an owner tracking it in PodSEO. A paid plan alone does not guarantee a card. Competitor tracking does not give you an owner's share control for that show.

The profile is public to anyone with its link. Its availability and the achievement shown can change as the show's eligibility changes; a downloaded image remains the image you saved. Use a fresh preview when promoting a new milestone.

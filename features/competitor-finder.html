Competitor Finder ​

Discover the Podcasts Really Fighting for Your Audience ​

Competitor Finder identifies podcasts competing for the same audience as your show — based on search rankings, platform recommendations, genre overlap, and visibility — not just by category. Instead of a flat list, it ranks competitors by how much of a competitive threat they pose, so you can see at a glance who's worth watching and who's worth tracking.

Competitors Ranked by Competitive Strength ​

At the top of the page you'll find a quick summary that frames your competitive landscape at a glance:

  • Top threats: Competitors with a competition strength of 60 or higher — the shows competing most directly for your search rankings, audience, and platform visibility
  • Rising competitors: Shows in the 35–59 strength range — moderate overlap in rankings, genre, or audience size that's worth keeping an eye on
  • Keyword battlegrounds: The total number of your tracked keywords where you face competition from other podcasts (contested keywords)
  • All competitors: A count of every competitor surfaced, ranked by relevance to your show (showing the most relevant)

Below the summary, each competitor appears as a card showing how many keywords you share, their visibility rank, the platforms where they overlap with you, and a threat-level badge (Top threat, Rising, or Emerging) so you can prioritize quickly.

Tracking a Competitor ​

When you find a podcast worth monitoring, use the Track action on its card. Tracking adds the competitor to your Competitive Analysis, where you can follow their visibility, keyword overlap, and activity over time. The number of competitors you can track depends on your plan.

When Strong Competitors Are Hard to Find ​

If your podcast is in a niche category or you're tracking only a few keywords, Competitor Finder may not find strong direct competitors right away. When that happens, it relaxes its filters and surfaces emerging competitors — podcasts with potential competitive relevance — and lets you know it has expanded the search. The best way to surface stronger competitors is to add more keywords for your show to compete on.

If no competitors meet the relevance threshold at all, you'll see options to Add more keywords or Refresh analysis so you can broaden your search and try again.

How Keyword Overlap Discovery Works ​

Platform-Specific Search Engine Analysis ​

Each podcasting platform uses different search algorithms and ranking systems, which means your keyword competitors vary significantly by platform:

  • Apple Podcasts Search: Uses Apple's proprietary search algorithm that weighs title, description, and category relevance differently than other platforms
  • Spotify Search: Leverages Spotify's music search technology adapted for podcasts, creating unique keyword ranking patterns
  • Amazon Music Search: Integrates with Amazon's broader search ecosystem, influencing which podcasts appear for specific terms
  • YouTube Music Search: Combines video and audio search signals, often surfacing different competitors than audio-only platforms

Understanding Keyword Overlap ​

Competitor Finder analyzes which podcasts appear in search results for the same keywords as your podcast, showing you:

  • Direct Keyword Competition: Podcasts ranking for your exact target keywords
  • Search Result Proximity: Shows appearing near your podcast in search results
  • Platform Variations: How competitor sets differ across Apple Podcasts, Spotify, Amazon Music, and YouTube Music
  • Ranking Performance: Which competitors consistently outrank you for shared keywords

Why Different Search Engines Matter ​

Search Algorithm Variations Create Different Competitive Landscapes ​

Understanding that each platform has distinct search behavior is crucial for effective competitor analysis:

  • Algorithm Differences: Apple Podcasts might prioritize podcast titles, while Spotify emphasizes description keywords, leading to completely different competitor sets
  • Ranking Factors: YouTube Music considers video engagement signals, Amazon Music integrates shopping behavior data, creating unique keyword competition patterns
  • Search Intent Variations: Users search differently on each platform - entertainment vs. educational content discovery varies by platform
  • Content Discovery Mechanisms: Platform recommendation engines influence which podcasts appear together in search results

Strategic Keyword Overlap Analysis ​

For each platform, Competitor Finder shows you:

  1. Primary Keyword Competitors: Podcasts ranking in the top positions for your main target keywords
  2. Secondary Keyword Overlap: Shows competing for related terms that could expand your visibility
  3. Ranking Gap Analysis: Identifies competitors consistently outranking you for shared keywords
  4. Platform-Specific Opportunities: Keywords where you face different competition levels across platforms

Competitor Selection Based on Keyword Performance ​

Focus on competitors who:

  • Consistently Outrank You: For keywords where you want to improve position
  • Target Similar Keywords: Shows competing for the same search terms
  • Excel on Specific Platforms: Where they rank better than you for shared keywords
  • Gap Opportunities: Where they rank well but you don't appear at all

Leveraging Keyword Overlap Intelligence ​

Cross-Platform Competitive Strategy ​

Once you identify competitors through keyword overlap, use this intelligence strategically:

  • Platform-Specific Optimization: Understand why competitors rank better on specific platforms and adapt your strategy accordingly
  • Keyword Gap Analysis: Identify keywords where competitors rank well but you don't appear, revealing expansion opportunities
  • Content Strategy Insights: Analyze how competitors structure titles and descriptions to rank for shared keywords
  • Platform Prioritization: Focus optimization efforts on platforms where you face the weakest keyword competition

Understanding Competitive Keyword Patterns ​

Track how keyword overlap changes over time:

  • Seasonal Variations: Some competitors may rank better for certain keywords during specific periods
  • Content Strategy Impact: Monitor how competitor metadata changes affect their keyword rankings
  • New Competitor Emergence: Regular keyword overlap analysis reveals new podcasts entering your competitive space
  • Platform Algorithm Updates: Changes in search engine behavior can shift your competitive landscape

Best Practices for Keyword-Based Competitor Analysis ​

Platform-by-Platform Analysis ​

  • Apple Podcasts: Focus on title-heavy competitors since Apple's algorithm heavily weighs podcast titles
  • Spotify: Pay attention to description optimization strategies since Spotify's search emphasizes episode and podcast descriptions
  • Amazon Music: Consider competitors who leverage Amazon's ecosystem keywords and shopping-related terms
  • YouTube Music: Analyze video podcast competitors who may have advantages in YouTube's search algorithm

Strategic Competitor Selection ​

  • Keyword Relevance First: Prioritize competitors ranking for your most important target keywords
  • Platform Performance: Track competitors who consistently outrank you on specific platforms
  • Ranking Consistency: Focus on competitors who maintain strong keyword positions over time
  • Growth Patterns: Monitor competitors showing improvement in keyword rankings to learn from their strategies

Maximizing Keyword Overlap Insights ​

Competitive Intelligence Through Search Performance ​

Use keyword overlap data to inform your optimization strategy:

  • Title Optimization: Learn from competitor titles that rank well for shared keywords
  • Description Strategies: Analyze how top-ranking competitors structure their descriptions
  • Category Selection: Understand how competitor category choices affect keyword rankings
  • Content Themes: Identify keyword patterns that suggest successful content strategies

Next Steps: Use your keyword overlap analysis to inform your Keyword Strategy and track competitor performance in the Competitive Analysis feature. This search-focused intelligence helps you make data-driven decisions about where to compete and how to optimize for better keyword rankings across all platforms.
